# Lexi research

Lexi research is architecture/reference work, not a data-ingestion lane.

The current comparative lexical-resource survey remains at `research/LEXICON_REFERENCE_SURVEY.md` and is linked here so the Lexi folder is the navigation front door without pretending external datasets are Lexi integrations.

## Why research is separate from integrations

An entry under `lexi/integrations/` means Gef has a defined runtime/build boundary to a canonical first-party graph or registry.

A resource listed in research has **no such implication**. Public or research access does not authorize copying lexical rows into Gef.

Before any external resource becomes an actual integration, a separate decision must establish at minimum:

- exact source and version;
- rights holder;
- commercial-use permission;
- redistribution/modification/database-right implications;
- attribution/share-alike requirements;
- whether data remains segregated or enters first-party storage;
- item-level provenance behavior;
- approval owner and review process.

Until that exists, external resources stay research references only.
