# Being grammar research — French (`fr`)

**Tier:** 1 — Full curriculum  
**Research quality:** Q3 deep research  
**Status:** queued  
**Parent:** `research/grammar/being/README.md`

## Goal
Produce a complete candidate inventory of how modern French expresses the Being domain, including copular `être`, existential `il y a`, locative/state constructions, and relevant alternatives.

## Required research
- [ ] Map identity, classification, property, state, resultant state, location, existence/presence, and other justified copular domains.
- [ ] Distinguish copular `être` from auxiliary uses in compound tenses/passive constructions.
- [ ] `il y a` and other existential/presence contrasts versus ordinary copular/location statements.
- [ ] Predicate adjective/noun agreement and complement behavior.
- [ ] Tense, negation, questions, pronoun/clitic effects relevant to learners.
- [ ] Important register/variety differences if pedagogically relevant.
- [ ] Common learner traps supported by sources.
- [ ] Gef corpus examples where available.
- [ ] Authoritative source map.

## Structured candidate output
For every rule record: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `morphosyntax`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

## Tier 1 completion bar
The research should support a complete French Being/copular lesson path without leaving a major subsystem unmapped.
