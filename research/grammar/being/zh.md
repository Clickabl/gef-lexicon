# Being grammar research — Mandarin Chinese (`zh`)

**Tier:** 2 — Selective lessons  
**Research quality:** Q2 standard research  
**Status:** queued  
**Parent:** `research/grammar/being/README.md`

## Goal
Map only the Mandarin constructions needed for the Being lesson. Research identity/equation, property predication, state, location, existence/presence, and relevant zero/non-copular strategies without assuming English `be` has one equivalent.

## Required research
- [ ] Copular/equative constructions such as those involving `是` and their limits.
- [ ] Adjectival/stative predication where an overt copula is not used.
- [ ] Location/existence constructions such as `在` / `有` where appropriate.
- [ ] Negation/question/aspect constraints that materially affect the lesson.
- [ ] Major learner traps and corpus examples.
- [ ] Authoritative source map.

## Output
Structured candidate mappings: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

Stop when this lesson can explain Mandarin accurately; do not expand into a complete Mandarin grammar curriculum.
