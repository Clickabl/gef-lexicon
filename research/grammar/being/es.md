# Being grammar research — Spanish (`es`)

**Tier:** 1 — Full curriculum  
**Research quality:** Q3 deep research  
**Status:** queued  
**Parent:** `research/grammar/being/README.md`

## Goal
Produce a complete candidate inventory of Spanish Being-domain grammar, including but not limited to `ser`, `estar`, `haber`, and relevant existential/state constructions. This must go beyond the oversimplified permanent/temporary mnemonic.

## Required research
- [ ] Map identity, classification, property, state, resultant state, location, existence/presence, origin/material/time/date and other genuinely related copular domains.
- [ ] Full `ser` vs `estar` decision boundaries and adjective meaning shifts.
- [ ] `hay/haber` versus `estar` for existence vs location of known entities.
- [ ] Tense/aspect interactions that materially affect the distinction.
- [ ] Agreement, negation, questions, impersonal/existential behavior.
- [ ] Dialect/register notes where real and pedagogically useful.
- [ ] Common learner traps supported by authoritative sources.
- [ ] Corpus examples, especially *Bremen Town Musicians*.
- [ ] Authoritative source map.

## Structured candidate output
For every rule record: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `morphosyntax`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

## Tier 1 completion bar
Enough coverage to design a complete Spanish `ser / estar / hay` learning sequence and later advanced refinements without discovering an unmodeled major subsystem.
