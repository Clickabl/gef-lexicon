**Tier:** 2 — Selective lessons

## Goal
Map the Indonesian constructions needed for the Being lesson, especially when copulas are absent/optional, when forms such as `adalah/ialah` occur, and how property/state, location, and existence are expressed.

## Required research
- [ ] Equative/classificatory copular constructions and omission conditions.
- [ ] Adjectival/property/state predication without unnecessary copulas.
- [ ] Location/existence/presence constructions and relevant prepositions/verbs.
- [ ] Formal versus colloquial/register differences relevant to the lesson.
- [ ] Negation/questions and major learner traps.
- [ ] Gef corpus examples and authoritative source map.

## Output
Structured candidate mappings: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `register_or_variety`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

Stop when the Being lesson can represent Indonesian accurately; no complete Indonesian grammar path is required.
