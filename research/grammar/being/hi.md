# Being grammar research — Hindi (`hi`)

**Tier:** 2 — Selective lessons  
**Research quality:** Q2 standard research  
**Status:** queued  
**Parent:** `research/grammar/being/README.md`

## Goal
Map the Hindi constructions needed for the Being lesson, including copular forms of `होना`, predicate adjective/noun behavior, state/location/existence, and constructions where English `be` maps to something else.

## Required research
- [ ] Present/past/future copular forms and agreement behavior.
- [ ] Identity/classification, property/state and location mappings.
- [ ] Existence/presence constructions and any possession-like interactions relevant to the semantic domain.
- [ ] Negation/questions and word order relevant to this lesson.
- [ ] Important constructions where English `be + adjective/state` is naturally expressed differently.
- [ ] Major learner traps and Gef corpus examples.
- [ ] Authoritative source map.

## Output
Structured candidate mappings: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

Stop when the Being lesson can represent Hindi accurately; no full Hindi grammar curriculum is required.
