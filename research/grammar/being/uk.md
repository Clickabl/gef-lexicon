# Being grammar research — Ukrainian (`uk`)

**Tier:** 2 — Selective lessons  
**Research quality:** Q2 standard research  
**Status:** queued  
**Parent:** `research/grammar/being/README.md`

## Goal
Map the Ukrainian constructions needed for the Being lesson, including present-tense copular omission/optionality where relevant, overt forms in other tenses, property/state predication, location and existence/presence.

## Required research
- [ ] Present copular/zero-copula patterns and actual conditions.
- [ ] Past/future copular forms and agreement.
- [ ] Identity/classification, property/state and location mappings.
- [ ] Existential/presence constructions and case behavior relevant to the lesson.
- [ ] Negation/questions and major learner traps.
- [ ] Do not inherit Russian rules; document Ukrainian independently.
- [ ] Gef corpus examples and authoritative source map.

## Output
Structured candidate mappings: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `morphosyntax`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

Stop when the Being lesson can represent Ukrainian accurately; no complete Ukrainian grammar path is required.
