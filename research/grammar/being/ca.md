# Being grammar research — Catalan (`ca`)

**Tier:** 2 — Selective lessons  
**Research quality:** Q2 standard research  
**Status:** queued  
**Parent:** `research/grammar/being/README.md`

## Goal
Map the Catalan constructions needed for the Being lesson, especially `ser/ésser`, `estar`, existence/presence, and the actual Catalan boundaries. Do not inherit Spanish rules.

## Required research
- [ ] Identity/classification, property/state, resultant state and location mappings.
- [ ] `ser/ésser` vs `estar` distribution in standard Catalan and meaningful variety differences.
- [ ] Existential/presence constructions.
- [ ] Predicate adjective/complement behavior, tense, negation and questions relevant to the lesson.
- [ ] Major learner traps, especially false transfer from Spanish.
- [ ] Gef corpus examples and authoritative source map.

## Output
Structured candidate mappings: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `register_or_variety`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

Stop when the Being lesson can represent Catalan accurately; no complete Catalan grammar path is required.
