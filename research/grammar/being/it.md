# Being grammar research — Italian (`it`)

**Tier:** 1 — Full curriculum  
**Research quality:** Q3 deep research  
**Status:** queued  
**Parent:** `research/grammar/being/README.md`

## Goal
Produce a complete candidate inventory of Italian Being-domain grammar, especially `essere`, `stare`, existential `esserci/c'è`, and the actual standard-vs-regional boundaries. Do not project Spanish `ser/estar` onto Italian.

## Required research
- [ ] Map identity, classification, property, state, resultant state, location, existence/presence, and other justified domains.
- [ ] Standard `essere` vs `stare` distribution and regional/colloquial variation.
- [ ] `c'è/ci sono` and related existential constructions.
- [ ] Copular vs auxiliary `essere` uses.
- [ ] Tense, agreement, negation, questions, complement behavior.
- [ ] Common learner traps supported by authoritative sources.
- [ ] Gef corpus examples where available.
- [ ] Authoritative source map.

## Structured candidate output
For every rule record: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `morphosyntax`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

## Tier 1 completion bar
Enough coverage to build a complete Italian Being/copular/existence path without treating Italian as Spanish with different vocabulary.
