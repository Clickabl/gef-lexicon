# `To be, or not to be` — title source

Canonical lesson title:

> **To be, or not to be**

Source work: William Shakespeare, *Hamlet*, Act 3, Scene 1.

## Edition policy

Shakespeare punctuation varies across editions. Gef's canonical punctuation for this lesson is pinned to the public-domain Project Gutenberg edition of *Hamlet* (ebook 1524), which prints the opening as:

> `To be, or not to be, that is the question:`

The lesson title uses the exact opening fragment `To be, or not to be` from that edition.

Folger's current editorial text instead prints `To be or not to be—that is the question:`. That is not a contradiction; it is an edition-level punctuation difference.
