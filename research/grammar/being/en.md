# Being grammar research — English (`en`)

**Tier:** 1 — Full curriculum  
**Research quality:** Q3 deep research  
**Status:** queued  
**Parent:** `research/grammar/being/README.md`

## Goal
Produce a complete candidate inventory of how modern English expresses the Being semantic domain. This is Tier 1 research, so cover the system deeply enough to support a full learner path, not only a comparison card.

## Required research
- [ ] Forms/constructions: copular `be`, existential `there be`, zero/ellipsis cases, and relevant alternatives.
- [ ] Map identity, classification, property, state, resultant state, location, existence/presence, and any justified additional slots.
- [ ] Keep auxiliary `be` for progressive/passive separate from copular rules.
- [ ] Tense/person/number forms and contraction/negation/question behavior relevant to learners.
- [ ] Predicate adjective/noun/prepositional complement behavior.
- [ ] Important dialect/register differences if pedagogically relevant.
- [ ] Common learner traps supported by sources.
- [ ] Gef corpus examples where available.
- [ ] Authoritative source map.

## Structured candidate output
For every rule record: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `morphosyntax`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

## Tier 1 completion bar
The research should be sufficient to design a complete English Being/copula lesson sequence without discovering an unmodeled major subsystem later.
