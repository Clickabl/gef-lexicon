# Being grammar research — Russian (`ru`)

**Tier:** 2 — Selective lessons

## Goal
Map the Russian constructions needed for the Being lesson, including present-tense zero-copula patterns, overt past/future copular forms, property/state predication, location, existence/presence, and case effects.

## Required research
- [ ] Present zero-copula conditions and when overt forms occur.
- [ ] Past/future `быть` behavior and agreement.
- [ ] Identity/classification, property/state and location mappings.
- [ ] Existential/presence constructions and relevant case choices.
- [ ] Negation/questions and major learner traps.
- [ ] Keep Russian independent from Ukrainian even where systems overlap.
- [ ] Gef corpus examples and authoritative source map.

## Output
Structured candidate mappings: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `morphosyntax`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

Stop when the Being lesson can represent Russian accurately; no complete Russian grammar path is required.
