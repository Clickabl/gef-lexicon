# Being grammar research — Arabic (`ar`)

**Tier:** 2 — Selective lessons  
**Research quality:** Q2 standard research  
**Status:** queued  
**Parent:** `research/grammar/being/README.md`

## Goal
Map the Arabic constructions needed for the Being lesson without pretending Arabic has one English-style copula. Cover present-tense zero-copula behavior, overt copular forms in other tenses/contexts, state/property predication, location, and existence/presence.

## Required research
- [ ] Nominal/adjectival present-tense predication and zero-copula conditions.
- [ ] Overt `كان`-family behavior where relevant to tense/state.
- [ ] Location/existence/presence constructions and definiteness effects.
- [ ] Negation, agreement, word order and dialect/MSA distinctions relevant to the lesson.
- [ ] Major learner traps and Gef corpus examples.
- [ ] Authoritative source map, clearly distinguishing MSA from dialect claims.

## Output
Structured candidate mappings: `form_or_construction`, `semantic_slots`, `conditions`, `exclusions`, `contrast_with`, `register_or_variety`, `examples`, `source_refs`, `review_state:candidate`.

Stop when the Being lesson can represent Arabic accurately; no complete Arabic grammar path is required.
