# Learning scenarios

`SCENARIO.*` files are authored situations that compose existing Gef learning-graph truth into something a learner can do, such as retelling a story event or ordering a drink.

A scenario is **not** a second linguistic truth system. It references canonical `TOPIC.*`, lexeme/sense, construction, and semantic-function IDs. Language-specific forms continue to live in `REAL.*` and the reusable lexicon. Games, quests, lessons, and story transfer are activities that can render a scenario; they do not own the knowledge.

This layer exists so a situation can be deliberately placed on a beginner path, surfaced from Lexi, queued by the learner, or suggested from a reader encounter without cloning grammar or vocabulary definitions.

Missing target realizations are normal. A scenario may exist before every language can render every activity. Runtime capability checks decide what can be shown.
