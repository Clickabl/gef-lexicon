# Single-target lesson policy

A concrete Gef lesson has exactly one destination / target language.

- `lesson.json.target_language` is singular and authoritative.
- A Spanish lesson teaches Spanish; a Portuguese lesson teaches Portuguese.
- Language-neutral semantic functions, curriculum concepts, pedagogical chunks, and lesson-family ideas may be shared across target-language lessons.
- Corpus evidence remains attached to the language in which it actually occurs.
- Support-language renderings explain the target-language lesson to the learner; they do not turn support languages into additional destination languages.
- Cross-language comparison may be a presentation or reusable family layer, but it must not blur which language the learner is practicing in a concrete lesson instance.

This keeps deterministic practice, hints, corpus annotations, and progress tracking unambiguous while allowing a small curated lesson catalog to reuse substantial underlying knowledge.
